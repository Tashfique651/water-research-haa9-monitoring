# Species-level UCMR4 addendum

## Methods

Species-level UCMR4 records were read from the Peterson et al. repository frozen at commit `e0dd19e8f392ac08aa3a00e6c09622014d074e7c`. The four FOIA workbooks contained 571,214 records from 4,924 public water systems. Events were linked to EPA's official UCMR4 HAA5, HAA6Br, and HAA9 rows using PWSID, facility, sampling point, sample ID, collection date, and sample-event code. Repository codes `SEH1` to `SEH4` were normalized to the official `SE1` to `SE4` convention. Analysis required all nine species and all three official group totals at the same event.

Official EPA group totals were primary. Species results marked `=` were represented by their reported concentration. Results marked `<` were interval-censored from 0 to the species MRL. The primary attribution assigned nondetects to zero, matching the public repository notebook. Sensitivity used MRL/2 substitution and species-specific interval bounds. For a species share, the lower bound divided that species' lower mass by its lower mass plus the upper mass of all other group members; the upper bound used its upper mass plus the lower mass of the others. Event-mass summaries weighted each complete event equally. Equal-system summaries first averaged concentrations within PWS and then weighted each PWS equally. Statistical intervals for primary shares used 10,000 PWS-cluster bootstrap replicates with seed 20260913.

Extra4 was defined as official HAA9 minus official HAA5 and attributed to BCAA, BDCAA, CDBAA, and TBAA. Brominated composition used official HAA6Br and the six brominated species MBAA, DBAA, BCAA, BDCAA, CDBAA, and TBAA. Group fractions were calculated only where official HAA9 was positive. Primary system fractions averaged eligible event fractions within PWS, matching the main UCMR4 analysis. Ratios of within-system mean group concentrations were retained as separate sensitivity fields.

## Results

The linked cohort contained 63,432 complete nine-species events from 4,924 systems spanning 2018-01-02 to 2021-12-13. Every complete event linked to all three official group totals. Literal equality is inappropriate for decimal sums stored as binary floating-point values and flags thousands of numerically negligible differences. With a 10^-9 µg/L tolerance, detect-only species sums reproduced the official results almost exactly (HAA5: 63,430/63,432 within 10^-9 µg/L; HAA6Br: 63,431/63,432 within 10^-9 µg/L; HAA9: 63,429/63,432 within 10^-9 µg/L). Only six of 190,296 group comparisons exceeded 10^-6 µg/L. Those six comparisons came from three physical samples, and each affected sample differed by exactly one relevant species MRL. Official group values were retained without alteration.

Among 61,520 events with positive official HAA9, the median Extra4/HAA9 fraction was 0.253 (IQR 0.134 to 0.400). The corresponding within-system mean event fraction was 0.287 (IQR 0.156 to 0.413; 4,840 systems). Event-mass contributions to official Extra4 were BCAA 46.1%, BDCAA 38.0%, CDBAA 12.8%, TBAA 3.1%. The species-specific censor intervals are reported in `species_group_contributions.csv`; they quantify unresolved sub-MRL mass rather than treating nondetects as observed zero.

The median official HAA6Br/HAA9 fraction was 0.299 at the event level and 0.371 after within-system averaging. Event-mass contributions to official HAA6Br were BCAA 38.1%, BDCAA 31.5%, DBAA 14.9%, CDBAA 10.6%, TBAA 2.6%, MBAA 2.3%. Results were reported separately under event and equal-system weighting because frequent samplers otherwise contribute more mass to national aggregates. These fractions describe the composition of measured HAAs, not toxicological contribution, health-risk coverage, exposure, or regulatory compliance.
